# Identity

- **Character name:** Virus Wild
- **Role:** Dominant spreading Wild mascot and highest-energy special symbol.
- **Personality:** Opportunistic, unstable, gleefully invasive, and impossible to contain.
- **Core emotion:** Explosive appetite.

# Visual Identity

- **Overall silhouette:** Jagged radial organism with a dense off-center core, seven unequal tendrils, and three blunt hooked spikes.
- **Body proportions:** Core occupies half the figure width; tendrils vary from short stubs to one long whipping arc; no bilateral symmetry.
- **Head proportions:** No human head; a single oversized facial cleft is embedded in the core.
- **Primary exaggerated feature:** One enormous corkscrew tendril sweeping upward and outward.
- **Secondary exaggerated feature:** Core split by a crescent facial cleft.
- **Asymmetry:** Core sits low-left; long tendril reaches upper-right; spikes cluster unevenly; facial features are displaced.
- **Materials:** Rubbery membrane, cloudy translucent sacs, oxidized crust, viscous chemical residue.
- **Surface condition:** Taut, cracked, wet-edged, and contaminated; stylized material, never biological gore.
- **Colour palette:** Blackened teal core, acidic yellow-green membrane, unnatural cyan highlights, muted violet-red accent, charcoal recesses.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Radial silhouette; corkscrew tendril; bright core cleft; displaced eyes; surface contamination.

# Facial Identity

- **Eye shape:** One small triangular eye near the upper-left core; one large compressed oval near the lower-right.
- **Mouth:** Wide crescent cleft with no realistic teeth.
- **Nose:** None.
- **Expression:** Ecstatic forward momentum.
- **Eyebrows:** No brows; two angled membrane ridges perform the same directional function.
- **Eye direction:** Both eyes converge on canvas upper-right.
- **Head tilt:** No separate head; the facial cleft rotates twenty-five degrees counterclockwise within the core.

# Slot Icon Composition

- **Camera angle:** Dynamic underside view looking up through the radial form.
- **Pose:** Core coils low-left while the long tendril lashes toward upper-right; shorter limbs recoil in alternating directions.
- **Weight distribution:** Dense at the low-left core, released explosively across the upper-right diagonal.
- **Arm placement:** Seven tendrils radiate unevenly; none form paired arms.
- **Hand placement:** No hands; tendril tips remain blunt, hooked, and clearly separated.
- **Leg placement:** No legs; two lower tendrils brace the core without resembling feet.
- **Silhouette readability:** Preserve core, seven distinct tendrils, three hooks, and the single long corkscrew arc.
- **Canvas occupancy:** 83 percent.
- **Safe margins:** Minimum 8 percent around every tendril tip.
- **Negative space:** Irregular wedges between every tendril; largest opening at upper-left.
- **Center of mass:** Lower-left of canvas center.
- **Icon readability:** Bright membrane edges carve the organism from transparency without a glow cloud.
- **Thumbnail readability:** Radial spread and corkscrew tendril must identify Virus Wild before its face.

# Pose Archetype

**Aggressive** — outward, directional, expanding energy without physical violence.

# Body Language

The entire radial body reaches beyond itself. Coiled and extended tendrils create a clear attack of space even if the face is hidden. Nothing droops or settles; every contour either launches or recoils.

# Behaviour

Freeze the instant the core uncoils and the longest tendril snaps toward a new target. Secondary tendrils lag behind, creating one readable burst without particles or motion trails.

# Visual Priorities

1. Explosive seven-tendril radial silhouette
2. Long upper-right corkscrew tendril
3. Dense low-left core
4. Crescent facial cleft and displaced eyes
5. Acidic membrane edges against dark teal mass
