# Identity

- **Character name:** Nurse
- **Role:** High-value vulnerable human mascot; care is communicated through excessive attention.
- **Personality:** Warm, socially fearless, improvisational, restless, and cheerfully intrusive.
- **Core emotion:** Eager fascination.

# Visual Identity

- **Overall silhouette:** Broad tilted crescent cap over a tiny forward head, pinched torso, and two unequal outward elbows.
- **Body proportions:** Compact adult body; oversized headgear; short torso; narrow waist; small forearms; large expressive hands.
- **Head proportions:** Small triangular head beneath a cap almost twice the head width; full uneven cheeks and pointed chin.
- **Primary exaggerated feature:** Impossibly wide fabric cap tilting toward canvas upper-right.
- **Secondary exaggerated feature:** Elbows spread like uneven wings around the compact torso.
- **Asymmetry:** Right cap lobe longer; left cheek higher; right elbow low and extended; left elbow high and folded.
- **Materials:** Thick faded fabric, waxy coated sleeves, dull rubberized mask, oxidized fasteners, cloudy glass.
- **Surface condition:** Repeatedly cleaned but stained; softened edges, warped seams, and restrained chemical residue.
- **Colour palette:** Dirty cream cap, faded blue-green tunic, petrol teal, charcoal, oxidized grey, warm skin, muted yellow-green stains.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Crescent cap; forward body wedge; unequal elbows; open face; lowered mask.

# Facial Identity

- **Eye shape:** Left eye round and fully open; right eye narrower and angled upward.
- **Mouth:** Wide compressed grin with closed lips and one higher corner.
- **Nose:** Tiny upturned wedge.
- **Expression:** Welcoming excitement held one beat too long.
- **Eyebrows:** Left brow forms a high arch; right brow is short and diagonal.
- **Eye direction:** Left eye looks at the viewer; right eye tracks toward canvas right.
- **Head tilt:** Ten degrees toward canvas left while the face projects forward.

# Slot Icon Composition

- **Camera angle:** Upper-torso, low three-quarter view from the character’s left.
- **Pose:** Head and cap lean toward the viewer ahead of the ribcage; elbows fan in opposite directions.
- **Weight distribution:** Forward and low, with the torso supported visually by the low right elbow.
- **Arm placement:** Left forearm rises vertically beside the face; right forearm opens horizontally away from the waist.
- **Hand placement:** Left hand hovers open beside the cap edge; right hand curls inward near the lower-right margin.
- **Leg placement:** Legs are outside the crop; the compact torso ends in an offset apron wedge.
- **Silhouette readability:** Cap, projecting face, raised left hand, and low right elbow must form four separate contour events.
- **Canvas occupancy:** 80 percent.
- **Safe margins:** Minimum 9 percent around cap tips, fingers, and elbows.
- **Negative space:** Large wedge between right arm and waist; small oval between left palm and cap.
- **Center of mass:** Slightly right and above canvas center.
- **Icon readability:** Light cap and face sit above a compact dark-teal torso.
- **Thumbnail readability:** The crescent cap and wing-like elbows must identify the Nurse before the lowered mask or eyes are visible.

# Pose Archetype

**Intrusive** — warmly entering the viewer’s space with open, unequal gestures.

# Body Language

The head arrives first, one palm is already raised, and the opposite elbow makes room for another action. Even with the face hidden, the forward body wedge and open hands communicate unstoppable helpful attention. The stance is buoyant, not threatening or controlled.

# Behaviour

Freeze the instant she notices a new detail and begins two helpful gestures at once. Her cap is still settling, one hand is lifting, and the other has not yet chosen what to adjust.

# Visual Priorities

1. Enormous off-axis crescent cap
2. Forward compact body wedge
3. Unequal wing-like elbows
4. Open split-direction gaze
5. Mask hanging under the chin

# Avoid

- Realistic nurse, believable uniform, ordinary cap, or clinical portrait
- Upright, balanced, passive, maternal, elegant, or symmetrical posture
- Surgeon-like hair crest, question-mark spine, crooked eyewear, pinching hand, or controlled smile
- Glamour, pin-up styling, childlike anatomy, anime, chibi, or doll symmetry
- Prominent syringe, scalpel, stethoscope, weapon, or procedural action
- Rage, cruelty, asylum stereotypes, vacant mania, or sinister authority
- Pure white, bright hospital blue, candy pink, gold, gems, polished plastic, or casino ornament
- Blood, wounds, gore, zombies, body horror, illness, or excessive infection marks
- Background, scenery, frame, typography, logo, particles, or environmental effects
