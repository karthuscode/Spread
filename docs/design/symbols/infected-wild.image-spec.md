# Identity

- **Character name:** Infected Wild
- **Role:** Transformed non-spreading Wild; a vulnerable human remnant overtaken by the Virus Wild family.
- **Personality:** Suspended, bewildered, depleted, and chemically altered.
- **Core emotion:** Arrested confusion.

# Visual Identity

- **Overall silhouette:** A folded humanoid cocoon: oversized bowed head, collapsed torso, arms wrapped in unequal arcs, and a compact acidic infection core visible at the chest.
- **Body proportions:** Large head; shrunken ribcage; long folded arms; legs fused visually into one narrow hanging wedge.
- **Head proportions:** Round human-derived cranium flattened on one side; tiny jaw tucked into the chest.
- **Primary exaggerated feature:** Two arms encircle the torso as an almost closed asymmetric cocoon.
- **Secondary exaggerated feature:** Small Virus-family core rupturing through the chest contour without gore.
- **Asymmetry:** Left arm forms a high outer arc; right arm crosses low; head collapses right; one shoulder remains human while the other forms a blunt infected nub.
- **Materials:** Worn civilian fabric, rubbery acidic membrane, cloudy residue, oxidized fasteners.
- **Surface condition:** Folded, stained, crusted, and chemically fused; no wounds, exposed anatomy, or decay.
- **Colour palette:** Charcoal and faded civilian violet, desaturated skin, dark teal membrane, acidic yellow-green core, muted cyan edge, minimal violet-red accent.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Cocoon silhouette; bowed human head; chest core; unequal wrapped arms; residual face.

# Facial Identity

- **Eye shape:** One human eye half-open and oval; the second reduced to a narrow membrane-covered slit.
- **Mouth:** Small slack diagonal line, closed and toothless.
- **Nose:** Soft bent wedge compressed against the cheek.
- **Expression:** Dazed interruption, not hunger or aggression.
- **Eyebrows:** One raised human brow; the other absent beneath membrane.
- **Eye direction:** Visible eye looks downward toward the chest core.
- **Head tilt:** Thirty degrees down and toward canvas right.

# Slot Icon Composition

- **Camera angle:** Seated-height rear-left three-quarter view, with the torso turned enough to reveal the face and chest core.
- **Pose:** Body folds inward around the core; head hangs right; shoulders rotate away from the viewer.
- **Weight distribution:** Suspended vertically through the low torso point; no planted stance.
- **Arm placement:** Left arm arcs over the core from above; right forearm wraps beneath it.
- **Hand placement:** Left hand disappears behind the opposite shoulder; right hand hangs open below the core with curled uneven fingers.
- **Leg placement:** Knees are drawn together and compressed into one tapering lower silhouette; feet are hidden.
- **Silhouette readability:** Preserve bowed head, high arm arc, compact torso core, dangling hand, and single lower wedge.
- **Canvas occupancy:** 81 percent.
- **Safe margins:** Minimum 9 percent around head, elbow arc, fingers, and lower taper.
- **Negative space:** One enclosed oval between arms and core; one narrow notch between head and high shoulder.
- **Center of mass:** Slightly below canvas center.
- **Icon readability:** Dark folded human mass contains one crisp acidic core; contamination stays attached to the body.
- **Thumbnail readability:** Cocoon posture and chest core must distinguish Infected Wild from the outward radial Virus Wild.

# Pose Archetype

**Arrested** — inward motion stopped and held around a contained transformation.

# Body Language

The body protects and imprisons the core at once. Wrapped arms, hidden feet, bowed head, and inward shoulders communicate depletion even if the face is covered. Unlike Virus Wild, no contour reaches outward or implies spreading.

# Behaviour

Freeze the instant after transformation finishes: the body has folded around the new core, one hand has lost its grip, and all outward movement has stopped.

# Visual Priorities

1. Inward humanoid cocoon silhouette
2. Contained acidic chest core
3. Bowed remnant human head
4. Unequal wrapped arms and dangling hand
5. Shared Virus-family dark teal and yellow-green membrane

# Avoid

- Outward radial tendrils, spreading pose, explosive energy, or Virus Wild silhouette
- Specific Masked Surgeon, Nurse, Human 2, or Human 3 costume signature
- Upright zombie, shambling corpse, aggressive attack, screaming, or generic monster
- Exposed organs, wounds, torn flesh, bones, blood, gore, body horror, or realistic infection
- Fully human normal state or completely non-human germ
- Teeth, claws, weapons, needles, medical procedure, or hospital imagery
- Neon aura, particles, VFX, liquid splash, scenery, frame, text, logo, or Wild lettering
- Gold, gemstones, casino luxury, photorealism, anime, chibi, or franchise imitation
