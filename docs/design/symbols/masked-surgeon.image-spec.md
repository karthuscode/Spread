# Identity

- **Character name:** Masked Surgeon
- **Role:** Highest-authority protected human mascot; medical role is secondary to eccentric preparedness.
- **Personality:** Obsessively precise, theatrical, calm, observant, and charismatic.
- **Core emotion:** Controlled delight.

# Visual Identity

- **Overall silhouette:** A tall crooked question mark: enormous off-center hair-and-face mass, hidden neck, one raised shoulder, one collapsed shoulder, and a narrow twisted torso.
- **Body proportions:** Oversized head; compressed ribcage; pinched waist; very long forearms and fingers; shoulders at visibly different heights.
- **Head proportions:** Head approximately half the visible figure height; broad upper cranium, enlarged cheek plane, narrow jaw.
- **Primary exaggerated feature:** Huge swept hair crest leaning toward canvas upper-left.
- **Secondary exaggerated feature:** Wide curved mask stretched by the smile.
- **Asymmetry:** Large open right eye behind an oversized crooked lens; smaller compressed left eye; right shoulder high; left shoulder low.
- **Materials:** Old black weave, dull rubber, waxy mask cloth, cloudy glass, oxidized steel.
- **Surface condition:** Handled, warped, chemically stained, and selectively damp; never sterile or uniformly dirty.
- **Colour palette:** Charcoal, black, deep desaturated green, muted teal, oxidized grey, pale living skin, restrained acidic-green residue.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Hair-and-head silhouette; crooked shoulders; smile-mask; mismatched eyes; tiny instruments.

# Facial Identity

- **Eye shape:** Right eye large and almond-shaped; left eye narrow and crescent-shaped.
- **Mouth:** Broad closed smile visible only as tension under the mask.
- **Nose:** Long crescent nose pressing visibly into the mask.
- **Expression:** Delighted calculation; funny before unsettling.
- **Eyebrows:** Right brow sharply raised; left brow angled downward.
- **Eye direction:** Both pupils fixed directly at the viewer.
- **Head tilt:** Fifteen degrees toward the low left shoulder while the chin turns toward viewer-right.

# Slot Icon Composition

- **Camera angle:** Upper-torso, high three-quarter view from the character’s right.
- **Pose:** Torso curves toward canvas right while the head counterbalances left.
- **Weight distribution:** Visual weight sits on the high right shoulder; the lower torso drifts off-axis.
- **Arm placement:** Right forearm crosses the lower torso diagonally; left elbow remains tucked behind the low shoulder.
- **Hand placement:** One long right gloved hand pinches a tiny instrument below the chin; the left hand is hidden.
- **Leg placement:** Legs are outside the crop; the torso taper implies a narrow planted base.
- **Silhouette readability:** Preserve uninterrupted hair crest, mask-cheek bulge, shoulder step, bent spine, and isolated pinching hand.
- **Canvas occupancy:** 82 percent.
- **Safe margins:** Minimum 8 percent around hair, elbow, glove, and collar.
- **Negative space:** One triangular opening between pinching hand and chest; one narrow crescent beside the low shoulder.
- **Center of mass:** Slightly above and left of canvas center.
- **Icon readability:** Pale face and teal mask separate from a single dark costume mass.
- **Thumbnail readability:** Hair crest, question-mark bend, shoulder mismatch, and smile-mask must survive at 64 pixels.

# Pose Archetype

**Controlled** — contained, deliberate, and off-balance without instability.

# Body Language

The lifted shoulder shields the jaw while the opposing shoulder falls away. The curved spine and delicately pinching hand show obsessive control without facial assistance. No limb reaches toward the viewer. The body appears to have paused by choice, not from uncertainty.

# Behaviour

Freeze the instant before he aligns a tiny instrument beneath his mask. The hand is precise, the head has already turned toward the viewer, and the torso remains calmly twisted as if continuous surrounding chaos does not matter.

# Visual Priorities

1. Off-center monumental hair crest
2. Question-mark torso and uneven shoulders
3. Stretched curved mask
4. Mismatched eyes behind crooked lenses
5. Long pinching glove and tiny instrument

# Avoid

- Ordinary or realistic surgeon anatomy
- Upright, heroic, symmetrical, or front-facing posture
- White coat, standard scrubs, stethoscope, head mirror, or accurate medical uniform
- Plague-doctor mask, armour, weapons, exposed needles, or scalpels
- Neutral professionalism, rage, menace, panic, or vacant madness
- Fully reflective lenses or hidden eyes
- Clean clinical materials, bright hospital blue, pure white, neon green, gold, gems, or casino ornament
- Photorealism, glamour, anime, chibi, superhero styling, or generic beauty
- Blood, wounds, gore, zombies, body horror, decay, or hospital horror
- Background, scenery, frame, typography, logo, particles, glow field, or cast shadow outside the figure
