# Identity

- **Character name:** Human 3
- **Role:** Vulnerable medium-value civilian mascot, visually equal in value and distinct from Human 2.
- **Personality:** Overconfident, confused, performative, and stubbornly self-assured.
- **Core emotion:** Misplaced certainty.

# Visual Identity

- **Overall silhouette:** A broad tilted bell: enormous barrel chest, tiny hips, short bowed legs, one elbow jutting upward, and a flattened sideways head.
- **Body proportions:** Wide upper torso; tiny pelvis; short thick arms; miniature bowed legs; oversized head seated on a long exposed neck.
- **Head proportions:** Wide flat cranium, compressed forehead, broad jaw, and one inflated cheek.
- **Primary exaggerated feature:** Chest expanded into a huge rigid barrel.
- **Secondary exaggerated feature:** Head stretched horizontally like a dented oval.
- **Asymmetry:** Left chest side fuller; right cheek inflated; left elbow high; right arm pinned low; coat hem longer on one side.
- **Materials:** Waxy synthetic coat, coarse undershirt, cracked plastic buckle, oxidized snaps.
- **Surface condition:** Over-brushed, stretched at the chest, puckered seams, oily edge wear, and isolated green residue.
- **Colour palette:** Blackened mustard, muted rust-violet, charcoal, dirty tan, oxidized grey, restrained acidic-green speck.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Barrel chest; horizontal head; high elbow; bowed feet; skeptical face.

# Facial Identity

- **Eye shape:** Left eye tiny and circular; right eye long and half-lidded.
- **Mouth:** Off-center pursed oval as if about to correct someone.
- **Nose:** Short broad nose pushed toward the small eye.
- **Expression:** Absolute confidence without understanding.
- **Eyebrows:** One thick level bar; one short brow raised at the outer edge.
- **Eye direction:** Small eye looks down at the viewer; long eye looks toward canvas right.
- **Head tilt:** Twenty degrees toward canvas right while the chest points left.

# Slot Icon Composition

- **Camera angle:** Full-figure frontal view from slightly below, with a mild clockwise Dutch tilt.
- **Pose:** Chest thrusts diagonally toward canvas left while the head tilts right and hips lag behind.
- **Weight distribution:** Heavy on the front left foot; right foot crosses lightly behind.
- **Arm placement:** Left elbow projects high and outward; right upper arm is clamped vertically against the ribs.
- **Hand placement:** Left palm presses flat to the inflated chest; right fist is hidden behind the hip.
- **Leg placement:** Short left leg planted wide; right leg crosses behind on the toe.
- **Silhouette readability:** Preserve bell-shaped chest, flat head, single high elbow, tiny crossed-leg base.
- **Canvas occupancy:** 84 percent.
- **Safe margins:** Minimum 8 percent around elbow, head, chest, and feet.
- **Negative space:** Sharp triangle beneath the high elbow; small diamond between crossed legs.
- **Center of mass:** Below center inside the oversized chest.
- **Icon readability:** Large mustard chest mass separates from the horizontal skin-toned head and tiny dark legs.
- **Thumbnail readability:** Barrel chest, sideways head, and high elbow must distinguish Human 3 from Human 2.

# Pose Archetype

**Defiant** — presenting certainty through expansion, display, and planted imbalance.

# Body Language

The chest claims space while one palm certifies its own authority. The crossed rear foot quietly contradicts that confidence, and the high elbow forms a boastful flag. With the face hidden, expansion above a precarious base communicates performative certainty.

# Behaviour

Freeze the instant Human 3 begins an unsolicited declaration: chest inhaled, palm already applied for emphasis, rear foot still crossing into an unnecessarily grand stance.

# Visual Priorities

1. Monumental barrel chest
2. Wide flattened head
3. High outward elbow
4. Palm pressed to chest
5. Tiny crossed bowed legs

# Avoid

- Long thin neck as the main silhouette, crouching, recoiling, or anxious body language
- Generic standing pose, balanced feet, heroic superhero stance, or military posture
- Same camera, hand placement, or energy as another human symbol
- Medical costume, protective mask, cap, tools, or professional cues
- Realistic anatomy, muscular idealization, glamour, anime, chibi, or conventional beauty
- Rage, physical threat, pointing at the viewer, clenched raised fist, or weapon
- Clean tailored clothing, gold, gems, glossy luxury, or casino styling
- Blood, gore, zombies, body horror, illness, wounds, or infection effects
- Background, scenery, frame, typography, logo, particles, or environmental effects
