# Identity

- **Character name:** Human 2
- **Role:** Vulnerable medium-value civilian mascot.
- **Personality:** Hypervigilant, cautious, indecisive, and physically unable to relax.
- **Core emotion:** Anticipatory anxiety.

# Visual Identity

- **Overall silhouette:** A narrow inverted hook: very long forward neck, small hunched torso, high rear shoulder, bent knees, and hands clasped behind the back.
- **Body proportions:** Thin compressed body; extremely long neck; short ribcage; long shins; tiny feet; oversized head.
- **Head proportions:** Tall narrow skull with a bulbous forehead, receding jaw, and one protruding ear.
- **Primary exaggerated feature:** Long swan-like neck projecting forward in an S-curve.
- **Secondary exaggerated feature:** Huge wedge-shaped forehead over a tiny chin.
- **Asymmetry:** Left shoulder raised behind the ear; right shoulder pulled back; one knee higher; one ear larger.
- **Materials:** Faded woven jacket, cracked vinyl collar, worn knit shirt, oxidized zipper.
- **Surface condition:** Creased, damp at edges, unevenly faded, and marked by small chemical rings.
- **Colour palette:** Desaturated aubergine, soot grey, muddy olive, faded brown, pale warm skin, tiny contaminated-cyan accent.
- **Visual hierarchy:** Neck hook; forehead wedge; crouched legs; hidden clasped hands; anxious face.

# Facial Identity

- **Eye shape:** Left eye tall and exposed; right eye compressed beneath the brow.
- **Mouth:** Tiny horizontal mouth pulled tightly toward one cheek.
- **Nose:** Long narrow nose pointing down and slightly forward.
- **Expression:** Listening for danger that has not arrived.
- **Eyebrows:** One high curved brow; one low straight brow.
- **Eye direction:** Both eyes glance sharply toward canvas upper-left.
- **Head tilt:** Chin pulled down while forehead cranes forward.

# Slot Icon Composition

- **Camera angle:** Knee-up near-profile view from the character’s left, eye level.
- **Pose:** Neck reaches left while hips and knees retreat right, creating an S-shaped countercurve.
- **Weight distribution:** Almost all weight rests on the rear right foot; the front left heel barely touches.
- **Arm placement:** Both arms are pulled tightly behind the torso.
- **Hand placement:** Hands clasp each other behind the lower back; no fingers interrupt the front silhouette.
- **Leg placement:** Right knee bends deeply under the body; left knee floats forward and inward.
- **Silhouette readability:** Preserve the long neck gap, forehead wedge, rear shoulder peak, and two unequal bent legs.
- **Canvas occupancy:** 78 percent.
- **Safe margins:** Minimum 10 percent around forehead, neck, knees, and rear elbow.
- **Negative space:** Large enclosed hook below the neck; narrow triangular gap between the knees.
- **Center of mass:** Left of center at head height, countered by hips at lower-right.
- **Icon readability:** Light head-and-neck shape contrasts against one dark narrow body mass.
- **Thumbnail readability:** The S-neck and recoiling knees must identify Human 2 before facial features.

# Pose Archetype

**Nervous** — recoiling while compulsively looking closer.

# Body Language

The body withdraws while the neck advances. Clasped hidden hands suppress action, bent knees prepare retreat, and the lifted shoulder protects the ear. With the face covered, the opposing head and hip directions still communicate anxious vigilance.

# Behaviour

Freeze the instant after a faint sound: the neck has stretched toward it, the feet have begun to retreat, and the hands lock together to prevent a startled gesture.
